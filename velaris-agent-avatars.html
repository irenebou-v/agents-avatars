Each dot lives on a fixed slot in the 12×12 lattice. A separate brightness field — one or two Gaussian hotspots travelling a closed path — drives each dot's glow independently of its position. Working lets the lattice flex vertically through a wave-height field (smooth, slow). Idle and Completed freeze the geometry and move only the glow gradient (purple #5358F8 / teal #0ABBB6). Needs attention pulses a ring outward from centre once per second in orange. Paused is still and desaturated. Failed scatters the dots off-slot with a subtle drift and faint red glow — the broken lattice.
